Switchwise — Support

Help with the Switchwise rail logic puzzle, on iPhone and Android

Contact

Found a bug, a level that looks wrong, or have an idea? Write to cucerenco.m23@gmail.com. Please mention your device and the level number.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap the switches to set where each one points, then press Go. The trains leave the depot one by one, and every train must reach the exit of its colour. A run that goes wrong costs nothing: change a switch and go again.

I’m stuck. Hint shows the right position of one switch: you get three free hints a day, and more from a short video or a hint pack. Show the run plays the first train of a solution after a video. Help never lowers your stars.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. On iPhone it is also kept in your iCloud, so it comes back on a new iPhone or after a reinstall.

I bought something on another phone. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases: regions and Supporter come back. Coins and hints are part of your progress, which follows you through iCloud on iPhone.

Can I change my ad consent? Yes: tap Privacy choices in Settings. It offers a choice where the law gives you one.

Why do I see no ads? Where the law asks for your consent and it was not given, the game shows no ads at all.
