Switchwise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Switchwise is a rail logic puzzle game for iPhone and Android, made by Maxim Cucerenco. This policy applies to both versions and explains what the game does — and does not do — with your data. Where the two differ, it says so.

Your progress: kept on your device

Everything Switchwise keeps about you — the levels you have cleared, your stars, coins and hints, the regions and trains you have opened, and your settings — is stored on your device. On iPhone, if iCloud is on, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account, so your progress follows you to a new iPhone or a reinstall; Apple stores that copy for you. Switchwise has no server of its own, so the developer cannot see how you play. Reset progress in the game’s Settings erases your progress from the device and, on iPhone, from iCloud.

The game does not ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone.

What Switchwise itself collects

Nothing. The game contains no analytics service and sends no data of its own to the developer or to anyone else.

Switchwise is built with the Unity engine. Unity’s own analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are switched off in the game. The Unity runtime may still send basic anonymous technical information about the device, such as its model, operating system version and hardware capabilities, as described in Unity’s privacy policy.

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, Switchwise signs you in to Apple’s Game Center if you use it. The game sends no scores to Game Center today. If leaderboards are added, this page will say what they show. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising

Switchwise is free. It shows a banner ad at the bottom of some screens, and a larger ad below the card that appears when you solve a level, always away from anything you can tap; ads never cover the puzzle, and there are no interstitial or pop-up ads anywhere in the game. You can also choose to watch a short video for a reward the game states first (such as a hint, coins, one more new level today, or an hour without banners); videos only ever play after you tap for them. All ads are served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK).

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device identifiers, the ads you have seen or tapped, how you use the game (such as launches and taps), and crash and performance diagnostics. Google may link some of it, such as identifiers, location and the ads you have seen, to your device. Google describes this in its privacy and terms and lists the companies it may work with in its advertising partners documentation.

Before any ad is requested, Switchwise asks Google’s User Messaging Platform whether consent is needed where you are. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in US states with their own privacy laws, you are asked first, and where consent is needed and not given the game shows no ads at all. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game’s Settings.

On iPhone, Switchwise does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so iOS does not give advertisers your device’s advertising identifier. On Android, Google may use your device’s advertising ID for ads; you can reset or delete it in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

Switchwise offers optional purchases, such as coins, hint packs, regions, a tip or a one-time Supporter purchase. On iPhone they are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system, and on Android through Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; the developer never sees or stores your payment details. The game keeps a record of each purchase’s transaction number on your device (and, on iPhone, in your iCloud copy of your progress), only so that a purchase is never delivered twice or lost.

Children

Switchwise has no chat, no accounts of its own and no links to social networks. The developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy — a new kind of ad, a new advertising partner, leaderboards, a new online feature, or analytics of any kind — this page is updated and the effective date above changes with it.
